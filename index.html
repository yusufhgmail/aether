Why your Aether is different

Get more done, your way.

An agent should fit your life and your work. Your Aether learns your preferences, builds the tools you need, and takes on more as you use it.

01

It adapts to you.

Shape how it talks, what it prioritizes, and how it works. Your Aether adapts to your preferences and instructions.

02

It learns automatically.

It notices your corrections, remembers what works, and uses those lessons the next time.

03

It builds new abilities by itself.

When your work needs a new ability, your Aether can create or improve a tool. You can inspect and change what it adds.

04

It works with other agents.

It can ask other agents for help and compare their work. You set what those agents may access and do.

05

It protects your private data in layers.

The part that learns and changes cannot rewrite the protected part. Checks can hold risky actions and record where data is going.

06

It becomes what your goals require.

As your goals change, your Aether can change with them. It is not limited to the abilities it started with.

An investment in yourself

The more you put into it, the more yours it becomes.

Your preferences, decisions, and useful tools add up. Aether is being built so you can keep that value, shape it, and choose what it runs on.

YOUR PRIVATE WORLD

Choose what you share.

Keep your agent’s memory on infrastructure you control. Choose which models and services it uses, and what they can access.

Hosted services still receive the data you send them. Self-hosting the agent does not make those services private.

YOUR WAY OF WORKING

Make it fit you.

Shape its personality, priorities, and instructions. When you need a new ability, your Aether can build it—and you can change what it builds.

YOUR LONG-TERM INVESTMENT

Keep what you build.

Keep your memory, working instructions, and tools on your own system. Back them up and keep improving them as your needs change.

We’re building toward changing models while keeping that work. Full portability is a design goal.

What complete ownership includes

Your stored data and memory. Your agent’s personality, instructions, and abilities. The software that runs it—sometimes called the harness. Its hosting, and ultimately the model itself.

Choosing a hosted model provider does not give you ownership of that model or remove its rules. Our goal is the option to run models you control, too. Exact public license and migration details will accompany the release.

Q&A

What does owning my Aether mean?

It means control of the agent’s stored memory, instructions, tools, software, and hosting. Aether’s mission extends to the model itself. Owning your agent software does not mean you own a model accessed through an API.

Can a model provider still limit my agent?

Yes. A hosted model provider can restrict or withdraw its own service. Provider choice reduces reliance on one service; it does not remove the provider’s rules. The long-term goal is the option to run models you control, subject to their licenses and your hardware.

Can I change models without starting over?

The public edition is intended to keep your stored memory and tools when you switch compatible models. This is a design goal, not a published migration guarantee. A different model may behave differently and need different resources.

When can I use Aether Many?

Aether Many is coming soon. It is under development, and the first public version will appear in the GitHub repository when it is ready. A working instance already runs its creator's life and business: managing email, investments, research, scheduling, and proactive monitoring from a VPS he controls.

What does “learns from you” actually mean?

Your Aether can remember your preferences, look back at past work, learn from your corrections, improve its instructions, and build reusable tools. It can still learn the wrong lesson, so important changes must stay visible and easy to correct.

What is Aether focused on?

An agent that learns your way of working, builds abilities for your goals, and keeps its memory and tools under your control. Aether’s mission extends that ownership to the software and ultimately the model, too.

How does your Aether protect private information?

Your Aether separates the part that learns from the part that protects you. It checks actions that can send data away, records important actions, and requires review before powerful changes. Connected AI models and tools can still send data away, so no system can remove every risk. These protections have not had a public security audit yet.

Will the public version be free and open source?

The Community Edition is planned to be free. Its license will be published with the public release.

Does your Aether require one AI model?

No. Your Aether can use different AI model providers. The public version is also meant to let you replace models without losing what your Aether has learned and built.

Why call it Aether Many?

Because each person and company should own the agent they depend on. The agents can develop differently and work together while remaining under their own owners’ control.
