What Aether does for you

It grows into the agent your goals need.

Aether does not stay as it was installed. It learns from working with you, changes what it can do, and becomes more useful over time.

01

It adapts to you.

You do not have to change how you work to fit the agent. Aether changes itself around the way you work.

02

It learns automatically.

It notices your corrections, remembers what works, and uses those lessons the next time.

03

It builds new abilities by itself.

It does not need every ability built in from the start. When something is missing, Aether can create or improve a tool. You can see what it added.

04

It works with other agents.

It can ask other agents for help, learn from what they return, compare their work, and bring the best result back to you.

05

It protects your private data in layers.

The part that learns and changes cannot rewrite the protected part. Checks can hold risky actions and record where data is going.

06

It becomes what your goals require.

As your goals change, Aether can change with them. It is not limited to the abilities it started with.

No connected AI can promise that private data will never leave your server. Aether’s protections reduce that risk and make important actions easier to see and control.

Safety in several layers

It can change itself. It cannot change everything.

The part of Aether that learns and builds is kept separate from the part that protects you. Ordinary self-improvement cannot rewrite those basic protections.

WHAT CAN CHANGEThe part that grows
  • Tools
  • Ways of working
  • What it remembers
  • Repeated tasks
  • How it uses other agents

This is where Aether learns, tries new ideas, fixes mistakes, and becomes more useful.

WHAT CANNOT CHANGEThe protected part
  • Basic conversation
  • Record of important actions
  • Checks before data leaves
  • A way for you to get back in
  • Review before powerful changes

Aether can use these protections, but the part that teaches itself cannot quietly rewrite them.

01

Checks before data leaves.

The private Aether records where it connects and can hold or block transfers that look risky.

02

Review before powerful changes.

Changes that affect the whole server must pass through a protected review and can be rolled back.

03

You can still get back in.

A built-in route stays available even if Aether breaks one of the tools or connections it added.

These protections reduce risk. They do not make any connected AI risk-free.

The private Aether has not had a public security audit. Connected AI models and tools can still send data away from your server. Stronger isolation is still planned.

Why “Many” is in the name

The future should have many different agents, owned by many different people.

Powerful agents should not all come from one company’s blueprint. Every person and company should be able to grow an agent around their own goals and keep control of what it becomes.

Those agents should be able to help one another and share what they learn without one platform owning all of them.

Questions you may have

What Aether can and cannot do today.

Can I install Aether Many today?

No. One private Aether is running, but the free public version is not ready. The code and installer will appear in the GitHub repository when they are ready.

What does “learns from you” actually mean?

Aether can remember your preferences, look back at past work, learn from your corrections, improve its instructions, and build reusable tools. It can still learn the wrong lesson, so important changes must stay visible and easy to correct.

How is this different from OpenClaw or Hermes?

OpenClaw and Hermes are mature agents you can install now. Hermes also learns and creates new skills. Aether brings its learning, self-built abilities, work with other agents, and layered protections together around one goal: becoming the particular agent its owner needs. The public version still has to prove that it can deliver this for other people.

Does running it on my server keep every secret private?

No. Running Aether on your own server gives you more control, but connected AI models and tools can still send data away. Aether adds checks, records, and limits around those actions. The current protections are incomplete and have not had a public security audit.

Will the public version be free and open source?

A free public GitHub release is the plan. The exact license has not been selected, so the site does not claim more than that yet.

Does Aether require one AI model?

No. The private Aether can use different AI model providers. The public version is also meant to let you replace models without losing what your agent has learned and built.

Why call it Aether Many?

Because the goal is not one perfect agent for everyone. It is many agents that grow differently, stay under their owners’ control, and help one another when useful.
