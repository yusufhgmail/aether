# Aether — Company Release Plan

_Updated: 2026-09-15 (Mexico City)._

**Direction:** Release Aether, build company AI workforces through consulting, then turn the repeated coordination work into a management product. Company-specific model training follows over the long term.

**Status:** Planned sequence. The public Community Edition is the first release; the service, management layer, and training system are future work. Dates and commercial terms are unset.

## Positioning

> Have your own sovereign AI workforce. Don't outsource your company's unique advantage.

The company should own the accumulated knowledge, workflows, tools, and operating system that make its workforce effective. Over time, it should also control the specialist model weights trained on its work. Its way of doing business should become an asset it can keep and improve.

Early releases may use external LLMs. Owning the agent runtime and stored knowledge does not remove dependence on those providers or keep data sent to them local. Model ownership is a later step in making the workforce sovereign.

**Competitor to track:** GrokBot, identified by Yusuf. Compare completed work, collaboration, human oversight, cost, and what the company can own and run independently. This is a comparison agenda, not a claim that Aether already wins.

## Release sequence

### 1. Release Aether

Publish a usable Community Edition that an external owner can install, give work to, and keep running. Establish a reliable individual agent that adapts to its owner's work and builds missing capabilities.

**Proof to establish:** External users complete useful recurring work with their own Aether. This is the foundation for putting several Aethers to work inside a company.

### 2. Build company workforces through consulting

Offer a hands-on service that builds multiple specialist **Aether employees** for a company. Define each employee's responsibilities, connect the relevant knowledge and tools, and make the employees collaborate on actual company work.

For example, a support Aether could identify a recurring customer problem, hand evidence to a product Aether, and ask an operations Aether to follow up on the resulting change. The company sets who owns each task and when humans must decide.

Specialization initially comes from roles, instructions, memory, tools, and workflows. It does not require a separately trained LLM for every employee.

**Why consulting comes first:** Real deployments reveal which roles, handoffs, failures, and supervision needs recur. Those lessons define the management product.

### 3. Build the management layer

Build a layer above the Aether employees that coordinates their work and makes the workforce understandable to humans.

- Show each employee's role, responsibilities, current work, results, and cost.
- Assign work, manage dependencies, and coordinate handoffs between employees.
- Show how employees collaborate: who requested work, who is responsible, what was exchanged, and what is blocked.
- Let humans set priorities, inspect decisions, approve actions, intervene, and stop work.

**Proof to establish:** A company can supervise several collaborating employees with less management effort while keeping clear responsibility for outcomes.

## Long-term direction

### 4. Train specialist LLMs for companies

Train or fine-tune company-controlled models for specific roles and tasks, using approved company examples and feedback from real work. Attach those models to the relevant Aether employees.

The aims are better task performance, lower cost, better collaboration, and clearer division of responsibility. Measure these against the best permitted alternative on the company's actual work; specialization is useful only where it improves the result or economics.

### 5. Automate decisions about specialist training

Build a system that uses workforce performance to decide which specialists are needed, what they should learn, which examples to train on, how to train and evaluate them, and when to retrain or replace them.

It should improve the workforce as a whole, including task boundaries and collaboration between specialists. Training and rollout operate within company-set data permissions, budgets, and quality requirements.

## Relationship to Enterprise LLM

Aether supplies the employees and their coordination; Enterprise LLM's company-specific training work could supply their specialist models.

The Aether project owns this full release plan. Enterprise LLM keeps a summary and link. Whether the commercial service becomes part of Works Like Us or remains a separate Aether offer is still open.
